Our team is made up of dedicated roles that work closely together to deliver high-quality custom sports kits. The Social Media team manages our online presence by showcasing designs, sharing updates and engaging with teams and supporters across platforms. The Design Team focuses on creating modern, eye-catching kit designs that reflect each club’s identity through colour choices, patterns and branding. Production is responsible for turning these designs into finished products, ensuring every kit meets high standards of quality, comfort and durability. Our Support team works directly with customers helping with enquiries, sizing orders and delivery ensuring a smooth and reliable experience from the first contact to the final delivery.
